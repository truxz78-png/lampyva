Why a firefly?

A firefly carries its own light and shines where everything is dark. That is what Lampyva does for your images: it brings out clarity, detail and new life. The name comes from Lampyris, the scientific name of the firefly, and viva — living light.

Lampyva works entirely on your own device. There are no servers that receive your photos, no accounts and no hidden fees.

Keep the light on

Lampyva is free and has no ads. If it helped you, a small donation keeps it online and pays for new features.
