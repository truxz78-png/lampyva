Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Lampyva ("we", "the site") is a free online image tool published by DriftforgeDev. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use the site.

1. Your images stay on your device

All image processing (upscaling, background removal, format conversion and batch processing) runs inside your own web browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers or to anyone else's. We cannot see, store or share your images.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, name or email address.
  • We do not use cookies of any kind, and no advertising.
  • We do not sell or rent any personal information.

3. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. AI models and libraries

The AI models and code libraries are stored on the same site (GitHub Pages) and downloaded by your browser when you use a tool. These downloads contain only the tools, never your images.

4b. Anonymous statistics

To know how many people use Lampyva and which features need improvement, the site counts visits and button clicks (for example "Donate", "Remove background", or when a tool had to use its backup mode). Each count contains only: the action, the page, the website you came from (if any), your browser language, your time zone and whether you use a phone or a computer. It contains no cookies, no identifiers, no IP address, no name or email, and never your images. The counts are sent to a small script run by Lampyva on Google Apps Script and stored in Lampyva's own spreadsheet; they are used only to improve the site and are deleted after about 13 months. Ad blockers may block it, and the site works the same either way.

5. Automatic translation

To show the site in your language, the text of the site's pages (not your images) may be sent to a translation service. No personal information is included.

6. Donations

Donations are handled entirely by the payment service you choose. With PayPal you leave Lampyva and PayPal's own privacy policy applies; we only receive the information PayPal shares with recipients of payments (such as your name, email and amount donated) and use it only to keep records of donations. Crypto donations are sent directly from your own wallet on a public blockchain; we do not receive your name or email.

7. Local storage

The site may save small settings in your browser (for example your preferred language). This information stays on your device and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

8. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions, ideas or problems? Write to norteviapagos@gmail.com or open an issue at github.com/truxz78-png/lampyva. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.
